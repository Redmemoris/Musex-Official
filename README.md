# Musex-Official

**Musex** is a music player for Android and Windows featuring a real vinyl turntable right on your screen. Download your favorite songs, organize them into groups, and enjoy listening with a spinning vinyl record, a moving tonearm, and the crackling sound of an old record.

## Features

- **Search and Download** from YouTube (including via direct links) and save songs as MP3 files on your device.
- **Previews** of any search result before downloading, plus recommendations based on your music.
- **Interactive Turntable**: drag the record to seek forward or backward, stop it with your finger for a "vinyl stop" effect, or flick it to switch tracks.
- **Synchronized Lyrics** that scroll as you listen, with built-in lyric search to find the correct lyrics.
- **Groups**, library search, endless shuffle, and song trimming for songs.
- **Synchronization** between phones and computers on the same Wi-Fi network: enjoy the same music and groups everywhere, with no cloud required.
- **Export and Import** your entire music library as a single file.

## Download

- **Android**: Download the arm64-v8a APK (compatible with most modern smartphones). The armeabi-v7a version is intended for older devices. If neither version works, install the universal APK.

- **Windows**: Download the ZIP file, extract its contents, and double-click musex.exe to launch the application.
